General

STACKLY is for anyone looking to build a career in technology. Whether you're a complete beginner, a self-taught coder looking for structure, or a professional looking to upskill, our programs are designed to take you to the next level.

No prior coding experience is required for our beginner-friendly bootcamps. We start from the fundamentals and gradually move to advanced concepts.

Learning

Yes, our core sessions are live and highly interactive, led by expert instructors. We also provide extensive recorded materials for self-paced learning.

Projects are evaluated through code reviews by senior mentors. We assess not just functionality, but also code quality, architecture, and best practices.

Career & Payment

Absolutely. Our career support team helps with resume optimization, mock interviews, and connects you directly with our 50+ hiring partners.

We offer upfront payment discounts, monthly installment plans, and Income Share Agreements (ISAs) for eligible students.
